# Module 2 – Heating station, storage cabinet and robot control

This page documents the update that

1. merged the hot plate and the stopwatch into **one combined heating station**,
2. added a **functional laboratory storage cabinet** with grabbable equipment,
3. repaired the **laboratory assistant robot and its control screen**.

Everything is generated by **VR Science Lab > Module 2 > Build Lab Scene**. After pulling this
update you must run that menu command again: the scene, the prefabs and the NavMesh are
generated files and are not stored in Git.

---

## 1. Combined heating station

One piece of equipment on the main workbench (`Instruments/HeatingStation`, prefab
`Prefabs/HeatingStation.prefab`). The old `HotPlate` and `Stopwatch` prefabs are deleted by the
builder.

```
HeatingStation                  CombinedHeatingStation   (facade, contains no logic)
├── Body, Foot x4, Vent x10     shared housing
├── StationStatusStrip          lit while the heater is on or the timer runs
├── HotPlate                    HotPlate + HeatingSurface + InstrumentStateVisuals
│   ├── CeramicTop, HeatingElement, HeatingRing x3, HeatLight
│   ├── HeatingZone             MeasurementZone (containers on the surface), ZoneRing
│   ├── Console, Display        temperature in °C + state line, StatusLed
│   ├── OnButton, OffButton, ResetButton
│   └── SampleAnchor            where "move to heating plate" puts the selected sample
└── Stopwatch                   StopwatchTimer + RotatableMount + InstrumentStateVisuals
    ├── TimerDeck, Swivel/Head  timer display (mm:ss.ff) + state line, StatusLed
    ├── StartButton, PauseButton, ResetButton
    └── TurnLeftButton, TurnRightButton   swivel the timer display
```

* **Independent logic.** `HotPlate.cs` and `StopwatchTimer.cs` are unchanged and do not know
  each other. `CombinedHeatingStation.cs` only holds references to both, drives the shared
  status strip and offers `SwitchEverythingOff()` and `ResetStation()`.
* **Heating:** ON starts heating at the configured rate (10 °C/s up to 300 °C), OFF stops the
  rise (the plate cools at 5 °C/s), RESET returns to 20 °C. The display always shows the current
  temperature.
* **Timer:** START, PAUSE (time kept), START again (resume), RESET (00:00.00).
* **Status indicators:** per instrument a status LED, a backlit display with a state line
  (`OFF`, `HEATING`, `MAX TEMP`, `OFF (COOLING)` / `IDLE`, `RUNNING`, `PAUSED`), a hint sign that
  follows the state, the glowing heating element, and the shared strip on the front edge.
* **Containers on the surface:** `HeatingSurface.cs` warms the `TemperatureSample` of a container
  standing on the plate: +2 °C per second while the plate is hotter than the sample, never
  above the plate temperature and never above 100 °C. Samples do not cool down. A container
  without a `TemperatureSample` (empty glassware) is detected but not heated.
* The names `HotPlate` and `Stopwatch` are kept for the two modules, so `LabDirectory`
  (GameObject.Find), the measurement log, the summary screen, the keyboard shortcuts F3-F8 and
  the reset controller work exactly as before.

## 2. Laboratory storage cabinet

The middle unit of the storage wall on the left (`Environment/Furniture/Storage Cabinets/Laboratory Cabinet`,
prefab `Prefabs/LaboratoryCabinet.prefab`), 0.90 m wide, 0.55 m deep, 2.25 m high.

* **Carcass:** closed base cabinet, back, sides and top with colliders, steel face frame,
  three storage levels (counter at 0.965 m, glass shelves at 1.385 m and 1.805 m). Every level
  has its own `BoxCollider` (2.5 cm thick, thicker than the visible glass) and a low front lip
  with a collider, so objects neither fall through nor roll out.
* **Doors:** two hinged glass doors (`Door Left`, `Door Right`) with steel frames, hinges and
  pull handles. Each door pivot has `CabinetDoor` + `XRSimpleInteractable` + a kinematic
  `Rigidbody` + colliders for the leaf and the handle. Point a controller ray at a door or its
  handle and press **Grip**: the door swings 105° around its hinge with a `Transform` rotation
  (0.9 s, ease in / out). Press again to close. Without a headset: left-click the door, or press
  **C** to open / close both.
* **Status:** the fascia shows `DOORS CLOSED | 23 / 23 STORED`; the count comes from a trigger
  zone covering the interior (`InteriorZone`, `MeasurementZone`), so taking an object out and
  putting it back is visible. The interior light brightens while a door is open.
* **Reset:** *RESET LABORATORY* (Home) returns every object to its shelf and closes the doors.

Contents (`Cabinet Labware`, prefabs in `Prefabs/Labware`), all with `Rigidbody`,
`XRGrabInteractable`, colliders, `Labware`, collision feedback and hover highlight:

| Level | Objects | Colliders | Mass read by the scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counter | Beaker 600 mL (empty), 2 x beaker 250 mL (distilled water 22 °C, saline 18 °C), beaker 100 mL (indicator 20 °C) | box | 190 g, 250 g, 270 g, 110 g |
| Counter | 2 specimen jars 120 mL, 3 sample vials (20 °C), 2 rolls of laboratory tape | box | 120 g, 20 g, 60 g |
| Shelf 1 | Test tube rack with six holes, 5 test tubes (12, 25, 37, 45, 60 °C) | rack: 3 boxes; tube: capsule | 250 g, 30 g |
| Shelf 1 | Erlenmeyer flasks 500 mL (empty), 250 mL (buffer 30 °C), 125 mL (culture medium 37 °C) | box + capsule | 210 g, 270 g, 150 g |
| Shelf 2 | 3 reagent bottles (ethanol, copper sulfate, iodine; 21 °C) | box | 350 g |

* **Test tube rack:** every hole is a `LabwareSocket` (an `XRSocketInteractor` that only accepts
  `Labware` of kind `TestTube`) with a trigger collider and an attach point at the bottom of the
  hole. Release a tube over a free hole and the socket takes it and holds it upright; grab it to
  take it out. The rack shows `5 / 6`. The rack itself can be grabbed and carried with its tubes.
* **Use with the instruments:** the scale reads the Rigidbody mass of any object placed on the
  pan; the thermometer reads the `TemperatureSample` of a filled container on its dock; a filled
  container on the heating surface warms up (section 1).

## 3. Robot and control screen

### What was wrong

| # | Cause | Effect | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The three screens had only a `TrackedDeviceGraphicRaycaster` and no event camera. In XR Interaction Toolkit 3.6 the simulator decides whether a mouse click is a UI press by probing the canvas with a standard pointer event; without a `GraphicRaycaster` the click is sent as a **grip** press, and a plain mouse click (no simulator) is not handled at all. | Clicking a screen button did nothing, on all three screens. The robot logic itself answered when called directly (keyboard, tests). | Every screen canvas now has `GraphicRaycaster` **and** `TrackedDeviceGraphicRaycaster` and an explicit event camera, exactly like XRI's own *GameObject > XR > UI Canvas*. |
| 2 | The equipment cart (a carving `NavMeshObstacle`) was parked 0.4 m from the *Analysis Desk* stop point; carving removes the NavMesh within the agent radius (0.5 m) of an obstacle. | That station was unreachable while the cart stood there (`Blocked`). | The cart now starts against the left wall, clear of every stop point; stop points keep at least 0.5 m from furniture and from the user's start position. |
| 3 | A station whose stop point is carved away (the user or the cart standing on it) made the robot report `Blocked`. | "Robot does not come" depending on where the user stands. | The robot aims for the nearest reachable point (`NavMesh.SamplePosition`) and parks next to the occupied stop. |
| 4 | When the robot was not on a NavMesh the command only logged a Console warning. | Buttons looked dead; nothing on the screen explained why. | Every command now reports its result on the screen (`GO TO DIGITAL SCALE > Driving to Digital Scale`, or `FAILED: ...` with the reason). The robot snaps itself onto the NavMesh when needed, and `NavMeshRuntimeBaker` builds the NavMesh at start if the baked data is missing. |
| 5 | The robot shortcut keys H and J are also simulator keys (toggle HMD, 2D axis). | Pressing them changed the simulator mode at the same time. | The shortcuts are now **X** (stop / resume) and **Z** (tour). |
| 6 | The test assembly definition used `UnityEngine.UI.Button` without referencing `UnityEngine.UI`; the runtime one used TextMeshPro texts (which derive from a `UnityEngine.UI` class) without it. | By Unity's assembly-definition rules this is a compile error in the test assembly, and a compile error anywhere blocks Play mode. | Reference added to both `.asmdef` files; `Tools/CompileCheck/check-asmdef-references.py` now checks it. |
| 7 | `Robot_DrivesToStationsAndCanBeStoppedAndResumed` parented its wall to the scaled ground before sizing it, so the wall became 4.8 m x 60 m and cut the floor in two. | The existing robot test could not pass. | The wall is sized in world space first. |

### How it works now

```
screen button (uGUI Button.onClick, persistent listener)
   └─> RobotControlPanel.GoToStation(index) / StopRobot() / ReturnToStart() / ...
          records "GO TO DIGITAL SCALE", shows it, logs it
          └─> LabAssistantRobot.GoToStation(index)
                 EnsureAgentReady()   agent enabled and on the NavMesh (snaps onto it if needed)
                 NavMesh.SamplePosition(station)   nearest reachable point
                 NavMeshAgent.SetDestination(...)  state Moving
                 Update(): remainingDistance <= stoppingDistance  ->  state Arrived, turns to face the station
          <─ onStatusChanged ─ the panel shows state, destination, distance and the robot's answer
```

* **Stations** (`Robot Stations/*`, index order is fixed): 0 Starting Position (charging dock),
  1 Digital Scale, 2 Heating Station, 3 Storage Cabinet, 4 Laboratory Workbench, 5 Analysis
  Desk, 6 Fume Hood.
* **Screen** (`Interface/Robot Console`): GO TO DIGITAL SCALE, GO TO HEATING STATION, GO TO
  STORAGE CABINET, GO TO LABORATORY WORKBENCH, STOP ROBOT, RETURN TO STARTING POSITION, plus
  RESUME, ANALYSIS DESK, FUME HOOD and AUTO TOUR.
* **Stop Robot** sets `NavMeshAgent.isStopped`, zeroes the velocity and cancels the tour; the
  state reads `STOPPED`. RESUME continues to the same destination; any GO TO button gives a new one.
* **Visual indication:** state / destination / distance tiles, the last command and the robot's
  answer, a NavMesh indicator, the destination button tinted green (red when blocked), a route
  line on the floor (`RobotPathIndicator`), the lit floor ring of the destination station, the
  robot's status ring (cyan idle, green moving, amber stopped, red blocked) and chest text.
* **NavMesh:** `NavMeshSurface` on `Environment` (children, physics colliders, Humanoid agent,
  voxel 0.05), baked by the builder into `Scenes/Module2_Lab_NavMesh.asset`. Furniture is marked
  *Not Walkable*; the cabinet doors are excluded from the bake; the user, the cart and the two
  screen stands carve the NavMesh at runtime.

## 5. How this update was verified

The update was written on a machine **without a Unity editor**. It was therefore verified in
two ways that do not need the editor, and it still has to be confirmed once inside Unity.

**Done, without Unity**

* `Tools/HeadlessSim` executes the real project code (runtime scripts, the scene builder and the
  PlayMode tests) against a functional stand-in for the Unity engine: scene graph with real
  transform math, component lifecycle, serialization, persistent `UnityEvent` listeners, trigger
  volumes, a grid NavMesh with path planning and carving obstacles, and emulated XR select /
  grab / socket and UI click. One run:
  * builds the laboratory with `Module2SceneBuilder.BuildLabScene()`: 0 errors;
  * plays the scene for 3 s: 0 exceptions, 0 errors, every Rigidbody resting on a surface;
  * runs **202 project checks** (`Harness/ProjectChecks.cs`): every Inspector reference of the 190
    Module 2 components, the wiring of every push button and of all 34 screen buttons, the
    station operated button by button, every cabinet object resting fully on a shelf without
    overlapping another, both doors swept through their swing without touching the neighbouring
    units, the robot stop or the contents, a test tube taken from the rack to the bench and put
    back into a free hole, a beaker carried to the scale, the thermometer and the heating
    surface and back, every robot station on the NavMesh with a complete path, every robot
    screen button clicked through the EventSystem path with the robot driving, stopping,
    resuming, re-routing and returning, the user standing on a stop point, the shortcuts and the
    laboratory reset;
  * runs all **42 PlayMode tests** (12 instruments, 16 laboratory systems, 14 new): 42 pass.
* `Tools/CompileCheck/check-asmdef-references.py`: every assembly definition references the
  assemblies its code uses.
* The API facts the fixes rely on (raycasters, XR Socket Interactor, Near-Far Interactor, the
  simulator's point-and-click, NavMeshSurface, NavMeshAgent) were checked against the XR
  Interaction Toolkit 3.6.1 and AI Navigation 2.0 package sources.

**Not verified here (needs Unity)**

* Compilation against the real Unity assemblies. On a PC with the editor, run
  `Tools/CompileCheck/RealApi/run-real-api-check.sh` (see `Tools/CompileCheck/README.md`), or
  simply open the project and look at the Console.
* Real physics (stacking, throwing, friction), real NavMesh baking and steering, real XR input,
  the look of the new models and materials, and the behaviour of the XR Socket Interactor.
* The stand-in is an approximation written without access to Unity. A pass there shows that the
  logic and the geometry are consistent; it does not replace the two steps below.

**To confirm in Unity (about five minutes)**

1. Run *Build Lab Scene*, then Window > General > Test Runner > PlayMode > Run All.
2. Press Play and go through the *Combined heating station, storage cabinet, robot control screen*
   tables of `TEST_CHECKLIST.md`.
